Financial Toolkit

Professional financial calculators for investment analysis, loans, and portfolio management

v1.0.0 — Built for BUS 201 / FIN 445 Coursework
📈

Compound Interest

Calculate future value of investments with compound interest, regular contributions, and varying frequencies.

🏠

Loan Amortization

Calculate mortgage payments, view amortization schedules, and analyze total interest costs.

💡 Tip: Making bi-weekly payments instead of monthly can save you significant interest and pay off your loan faster!
📊

Investment Returns

Calculate CAGR, annualized returns, and compare investment performance across time periods.

🏖️

Retirement Planner

Project retirement savings needs, calculate required monthly contributions, and test withdrawal strategies.

⚠️ 4% Rule: Traditional safe withdrawal rate is 3-4% of initial portfolio, adjusted annually for inflation.
📜

Bond Calculator

Calculate bond prices, yields, duration, and convexity for fixed income analysis.

💰

NPV / IRR Analysis

Evaluate investment projects using Net Present Value and Internal Rate of Return calculations.

📝 Note: IRR is calculated using numerical approximation. For non-conventional cash flows (multiple sign changes), there may be multiple IRRs.
